# Main figure legends

**Fig. 1 | Frozen cohort construction, 24-h landmark and database roles.** **a,** Prespecified MIMIC-IV development and eICU validation streams. The aligned primary phenotype comprised 434 MIMIC-IV stays before the landmark and 347 patients reaching 24 h (31 in-hospital deaths), and 470 eICU stays before the landmark and 359 patients reaching 24 h (21 deaths). The eICU broad and sensitive phenotype analyses comprised 1,264 to 1,007 patients (87 deaths) and 1,385 to 1,106 patients (96 deaths), respectively. The strict time-window phenotype is displayed once as NE/not executable because diagnosis event time is unavailable; it is not equated with the primary phenotype. **b,** NWICU was restricted to computability analysis, SICdb to exploratory analysis because fewer than 20 deaths were available, and HiRID to a general-ICU measurement-process reference. Counts are aggregate first eligible ICU stays/patients. Source data: `fig1_design_and_cohort_flow.csv`.

**Fig. 2 | Descriptive cross-database differences in the 0-24-h measurement process.** **a,** Percentage of patients with at least one eligible measure among frozen variables in the aligned primary-AP landmark cohorts (MIMIC-IV, n=347; eICU, n=359; events are not applicable to this descriptive process panel). **b,** Median measures per hour among observed patients; the colour scale uses log(1+rate) for display only. **c,** eICU-minus-MIMIC-IV difference in presence, in percentage points. NE denotes not estimable or not structurally comparable and is not zero measurement. These patterns do not identify clinician intent or a causal measurement-process effect. Source data: `fig2_measurement_process_shift.csv`.

**Fig. 3 | Corrected internal, locked external and phenotype-sensitivity performance.** **a-d,** AUROC, AUPRC, Brier score and log loss for Models A and B in corrected keyed MIMIC-IV out-of-fold predictions (n=347; 31 deaths), the locked primary eICU cohort (n=359; 21 deaths) and the post-audit sensitive eICU cohort (n=1,106; 96 deaths). Points show estimates and whiskers show 95% patient-bootstrap intervals. Cohort shapes distinguish corrected internal, primary locked external and sensitivity estimates. The post-audit sensitivity estimates are not a blind primary external validation and are not used to claim a formal primary degradation contrast. Source data: `fig3_primary_corrected_performance.csv`.

**Fig. 4 | External calibration and aggregate prediction distributions.** **a,b,** Standard binomial-logistic calibration intercept and slope for Models A and B in the locked primary and sensitive eICU cohorts; points show estimates and whiskers show 95% confidence intervals. **c,** Aggregate standard logistic-calibration curves for the locked primary eICU cohort (n=359; 21 deaths), with the ideal line, patient-bootstrap 95% bands and grouped observed-risk summaries with binomial intervals. **d,** Four aggregate predicted-risk summary rows by outcome; boxes show the interquartile range, centre lines the median and whiskers the 5th-95th percentiles. No patient-level prediction rows are used or redistributed. The low event count makes absolute-risk calibration uncertain. Source data: `fig4_calibration_parameters.csv`, `fig4_calibration_bins.csv`, `fig4_logistic_calibration_curves.csv` and `fig4_prediction_distribution_summary.csv`.

**Fig. 5 | Phenotype and clustering sensitivity: paired A-versus-B robustness.** **a,b,** AUROC and AUPRC for Models A and B in the locked primary eICU cohort (n=359; 21 deaths) and the sensitive phenotype cohort (n=1,106; 96 deaths). Thin intervals are patient-bootstrap 95% confidence intervals; broad translucent intervals are hospital-cluster 95% confidence intervals. **c,d,** Paired Model A-minus-Model B differences in AUROC and AUPRC. Phenotype choice changes estimates, whereas paired differences between Models A and B are small and uncertain. This is a robustness analysis, not a causal decomposition. Source data: `fig5_phenotype_cluster_paired_robustness.csv`.

Historical Stage-6B implementation audit—provenance only; not current decomposition estimates or effect analysis. The superseded Stage-6B implementation is retained only in the audit table and is not displayed as a main scientific figure.
