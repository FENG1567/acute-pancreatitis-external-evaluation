# Supplementary figure legends

**Fig. S1 | Exploratory external model metrics.** AUROC, AUPRC, Brier score, log loss and calibration slope for exploratory Model C and the paper-guided reconstruction in the locked eICU cohort (n=359; 21 deaths). Points show estimates and whiskers show 95% patient-bootstrap confidence intervals. Metrics use different axes and must not be ranked across panels. These exploratory models do not replace Models A and B as the primary analyses. Source data: `figS1_exploratory_models.csv`.

**Fig. S2 | Supportive/exploratory decision-curve analysis.** Net benefit from threshold 0 to 0.30 in the locked eICU cohort (n=359; 21 deaths) for Models A and B, treat-all and treat-none strategies. The small event count limits precision; these curves are supportive and do not establish clinical utility. Source data: `figS2_decision_curve.csv`.

**Fig. S3 | Corrected keyed out-of-fold heterogeneity.** **a-d,** AUROC, AUPRC, Brier score and log loss for Models A and B in three held-out outer folds. Fold 0 contained 116 patients/10 deaths, Fold 1 contained 116/11 and Fold 2 contained 115/10 (total n=347; 31 deaths). Pooled keyed out-of-fold predictions have been reconstructed; these panels show fold heterogeneity rather than replacing the pooled estimates. Source data: `figS3_corrected_outer_fold_performance.csv`.
